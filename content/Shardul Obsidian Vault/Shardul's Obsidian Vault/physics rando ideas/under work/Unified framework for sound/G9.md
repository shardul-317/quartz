### **G9: Aeroelastic Flutter**

**Objective**: Model the dynamic interaction between fluid flow and flexible structures to predict aeroelastic flutter.

---

##### **3.9.1 Governing Equations**

1. **Structural Dynamics Equation**:  
   For a flexible structure, the equation of motion is:  
   $$
   m \ddot{w} + c \dot{w} + k w = F_{\text{fluid}}(w),
$$  
   where $w$ is the displacement, $m$ is the mass, $c$ is the damping coefficient, and $k$ is the stiffness.

2. **Fluid-Structure Interaction**:  
   The aerodynamic force $F_{\text{fluid}}$ is obtained by integrating the pressure distribution over the structural surface:  
   $$
   F_{\text{fluid}} = \int_{\text{surface}} p \, dS.
$$  

3. **Reduced Frequency**:  
   The dimensionless reduced frequency $k$ is defined as:  
   $$
   k = \frac{\omega b}{V},
$$  
   where $\omega$ is the angular frequency, $b$ is the characteristic length, and $V$ is the flow velocity.

---

##### **3.9.2 Physical Interpretation**

1. **Flutter Mechanism**:  
   Aeroelastic flutter occurs when the energy input from the fluid flow exceeds the energy dissipated by structural damping, leading to self-sustained oscillations.

2. **Critical Flutter Velocity**:  
   The velocity at which flutter occurs is determined by solving the eigenvalue problem:  
   $$
   \det\left( \frac{V^2}{L^2} M + K - \frac{1}{2} \rho V^2 (Q_R + p Q_I) \right) = 0,
$$  
   where $M$ and $K$ are the mass and stiffness matrices, $\rho$ is the fluid density, and $Q_R$, $Q_I$ are the real and imaginary parts of the aerodynamic force matrix.

---

##### **3.9.3 Dimensional Analysis**

1. **Structural Terms**:  
   $$
   [m] = \text{kg}, \quad [c] = \text{kg/s}, \quad [k] = \text{N/m}.
$$  

2. **Aerodynamic Force**:  
   $$
   [F_{\text{fluid}}] = \text{N}.
$$  

3. **Reduced Frequency**:  
   $$
   [k] = \text{dimensionless}.
$$  

---

##### **3.9.4 Case Study: Cantilevered Plate in Axial Flow**

1. **Problem Setup**:  
   Analyze flutter of a cantilevered plate in a uniform flow.

2. **Parameters**:  
   - Plate length: $b = 0.1 \, \text{m}$  
   - Flow velocity: $V = 10 \, \text{m/s}$  
   - Air density: $\rho = 1.225 \, \text{kg/m}^3$  
   - Plate stiffness: $k = 1000 \, \text{N/m}$  

3. **Calculation**:  
   The critical flutter velocity can be estimated using:  
   $$
   V_f = \sqrt{\frac{2k}{\rho S}},
$$  
   where $S$ is the reference area.

4. **Result**:  
   For a plate with reference area $S = 0.01 \, \text{m}^2$:  
   $$
   V_f = \sqrt{\frac{2 \times 1000}{1.225 \times 0.01}} \approx 12.91 \, \text{m/s}.
$$  

---

##### **3.9.5 Limitations**

1. **Linear Assumption**:  
   The derivation assumes small displacements and linear structural behavior.

2. **Simplified Fluid Model**:  
   The model often assumes inviscid flow, neglecting viscous effects that can be significant in real-world applications.

3. **Neglected Nonlinearities**:  
   The model does not account for geometric or aerodynamic nonlinearities that can occur at large deflections.
