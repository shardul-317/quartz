#### **Physical Interpretation of the Two Solutions**

- **High-Angle Trajectory** ($+$ root):
  - **Mechanics**: Larger vertical velocity ($v_0 \sin\theta$) dominates, creating a steep arc.

- **Low-Angle Trajectory** ($-$ root):
  - **Mechanics**: Larger horizontal velocity ($v_0 \cos\theta$) prioritizes speed.
  

***Discriminant Condition to maintain Physical Meaning***
   - When the discriminant is zero ($\theta_{\text{max}}$), it represents the the optimal balance between high and low trajectories, leading to *MAXIMUM RANGE*
   - The initial velocity $v_0$ must exceed a threshold to reach the target. If violated, the hoop is **unreachable**.$$
   v_0^4 - 2 g h v_0^2 - g^2 R^2 \geq 0.
$$
**Time-of-Flight Flexibility**:
   - **High-angle**: Longer air time ($t_{\text{high}}$) allows gravity to pull the ball down to $h$.
   - **Low-angle**: Shorter air time ($t_{\text{low}}$) due to horizontal speed.
**Symmetry**
   - When ($h = 0$), the formulas reduces to $\theta = \frac{1}{2} \arcsin\left(\frac{g R}{v_0^2}\right)$, this pertains to a special case with symmetric angles $\theta$ and $90^\circ - \theta$
