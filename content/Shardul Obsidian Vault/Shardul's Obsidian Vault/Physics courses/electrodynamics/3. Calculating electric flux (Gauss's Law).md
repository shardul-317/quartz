So considering electric flux on a sphere 
![[Pasted image 20250305194416.png]]

notice how both vectors are perpendicular to the surface, and understand how is makes sense physically.

after understanding that, come to how we calculate the total flux flowing through the circle/sphere, it is given by 
$$\phi E= \oint \overrightarrow E \cdot \overrightarrow A$$

and as in this case the dot product between the vectors comes to 1 as they are facing the same direction we can rewrite this as $$\phi E= \oint E  A$$
further as remembering from [[1. Field strength of an object with dimensions]], the field strength (in this case) is constant and can be taken out from the equation. Further it would then become the integral of dA which would become A. Resulting in this equation
$$\phi E=  E  A$$
Substituting with values of E and A  (sphere), this can be understood/found.

$$ \phi E=k Q4 \pi$$
this is also written as
$$ \phi E= \frac {Q}{\epsilon_0}$$
because $$k4\pi=\frac{1}{\epsilon/0}$$
this is then called Gauss's Law, and works on any closed/Gaussian surface. it could be any curve... yes any (just needs to be closed).
