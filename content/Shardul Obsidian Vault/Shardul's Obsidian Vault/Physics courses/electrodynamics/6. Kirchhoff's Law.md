
The Kirchhoff Law's are as follows
- Sum of current in should be equal to the cum of current out of a junction

![[Pasted image 20250306154211.png]]

- If you start from point a and comeback to point a through whatever path your potential energy must be the same. (this definitely applies to a circuit as it is a loop)
![[Pasted image 20250306154419.png]]

Much complex circuits can be solved using a manipulation of these two rules
