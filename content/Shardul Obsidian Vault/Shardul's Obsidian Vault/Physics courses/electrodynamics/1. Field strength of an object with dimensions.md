the object has certain dimensions (could be curved, [[Calculus]]) and has a charge density. Field strength is given by,
$$
\overrightarrow E = \frac {Q}{r^2}     \hat r
$$
so the total field strength of the object can be given by adding up all the little field strength of all the charges in the object. Which is why wedivide total charge into infinitesmially small charges
 $$
\overrightarrow  E_{total}=\int\overrightarrow E_{little} =\int \frac {dQ}{r^2}     \hat r

 $$
and as  charge density is $$ \sigma = \frac{Q}{L}$$
so
$$dQ= \sigma d(L)$$
Where L is length, and so dQ can essentialy be replaced by this.
